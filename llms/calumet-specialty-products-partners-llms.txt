# Calumet, Inc. (Calumet Specialty Products Partners)

> Calumet, Inc. (NASDAQ: CLMT) is a publicly traded U.S. specialty-hydrocarbon
> manufacturer founded in 1919 and headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. It
> formulates and sells lubricating oils, solvents, waxes, esters, packaged and
> synthetic specialty products, asphalt, fuels and renewable diesel from 12 North
> American production facilities to roughly 2,700 customers in more than 90
> countries, under the Calumet, Royal Purple, Bel-Ray, Penreco, Orchex and TruFuel
> brands.

## API surface

Calumet publishes **no public API and no developer program.** Verified 2026-09-06
against the registrable domain, the four brand hosts and the investor-relations
host:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, gRPC, WSDL, AsyncAPI or OGC contract at any
  probed location (`/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs` all 404
  on calumet.com).
- No API subdomain exists: `api.calumet.com`, `developer.calumet.com`,
  `developers.calumet.com`, `portal.calumet.com`, `orders.calumet.com` and
  `sds.calumet.com` are all NXDOMAIN.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery document of any kind on any host — no security.txt,
  no OpenID/OAuth metadata, no api-catalog, no A2A agent card.
- No hosted or stdio MCP server, and no first-party SDK on npm, PyPI, RubyGems or
  crates.io.
- The only machine-readable HTTP surface on calumet.com is the stock WordPress
  REST namespace at https://calumet.com/wp-json/ — the marketing site's own CMS
  and its plugins (Gravity Forms, Popup Maker, Yoast, Wordfence, Redirection), not
  a Calumet product API. It is not listed as an API in this profile.

Commercial integration with Calumet is a human and document motion: credit
applications, tax certificates, terms of sale and sample requests, handled through
the pages below and through the sales organization.
